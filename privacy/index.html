Introduction

Starman Inc. ("Company", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect information when you use our software-as-a-service platform (the "Service").

The Service may provide:

  • Unified Inbox
  • Live Chat
  • AI-Assisted Draft Generation
  • Email Synchronization
  • Calendar Synchronization
  • Customer Communication Management
  • Workflow Automation
  • Team Collaboration Features

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

How We Use Information

We use information solely to provide, maintain, improve, and secure the Service.

Purposes include:

  • User authentication
  • Account management
  • Unified inbox synchronization
  • Email synchronization
  • Calendar synchronization
  • Live chat functionality
  • AI-assisted draft generation
  • Search and indexing
  • Customer support
  • Security monitoring
  • Fraud prevention
  • Product performance monitoring

We do not use customer content for advertising purposes.

The Service may use artificial intelligence technologies to:

  • Generate draft responses

  • Recommend actions
  • Improve workflow efficiency

Customer Content may be processed by AI systems only to provide requested functionality.

Users remain responsible for reviewing AI-generated content before sending, publishing, or relying on such content.

AI-generated outputs may contain inaccuracies, omissions, or errors.

AI Training Policy

Unless explicitly authorized by the customer:

  • Customer Content is not sold.
  • Customer Content is not licensed for advertising purposes.
  • Customer Content is not shared with unrelated third parties for model training.

Any future use of customer content for model training would require explicit customer consent.
